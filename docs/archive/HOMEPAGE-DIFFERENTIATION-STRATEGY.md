**Risk Identified:**
> "The analysis focuses heavily on visual patterns from competitors, but FlipOps' core differentiator isn't visual — it's algorithmic intelligence (distress scoring, guardrails, automation). **FlipOps IS:**
- ✅ **Decision-making platform** that happens to have CRM features
- ✅ **Algorithmic intelligence system** that protects profit margins
- ✅ **Automated guardrails** that prevent costly mistakes

**The fundamental shift:**
- ❌ OLD: "FlipOps is real estate investment software (like REsimpli/PropStream/FlipperForce)"
